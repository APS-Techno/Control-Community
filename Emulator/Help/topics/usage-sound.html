Sound Effects

The Emulator can produce synthesized sound effects that mimic the audible feedback of the real vacuum system hardware. Sounds are triggered by state changes in valves and pumps during simulation or log replay, and by stepper/stage motion during live simulation.

Sound Table

EventSoundDescription
Valve open Pneumatic chpok 120 Hz tone burst, 100ms duration — simulates the pneumatic actuator firing to open a solenoid valve
Valve close Snap 55ms duration — simulates the spring-return snap of a solenoid valve closing
Pump on Rising tone Frequency sweep from 280 Hz to 310 Hz over 180ms — simulates a scroll pump motor spinning up
Pump off Falling tone Frequency sweep from 220 Hz to 130 Hz over 150ms — simulates a scroll pump motor winding down
Motor moving Stepper whine Continuous 600 Hz buzzing loop while any stepper drive or stage is moving (including Seek Home); stops when all motion completes

Enabling Sound

Sound effects are controlled by the Sound checkbox on the Log Replay tab. When checked, sounds play during both live simulation and log replay whenever a valve or pump changes state, and while motors are moving.

Technical Details

All sounds are synthesized in real-time by the SoundManager — no external audio files are used. The synthesis parameters:

  • Format: 16-bit mono PCM
  • Sample rate: 22,050 Hz
  • Generation: waveforms are computed sample-by-sample and played via the Windows audio API

Because sounds are synthesized programmatically, they have minimal memory footprint and no dependency on external sound files.
